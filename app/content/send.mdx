## The product challenge

Send was making onchain money move like a consumer product. The hard part was not any single screen. It was joining wallet ownership, KYC, card issuance, funding, authentication, transaction state, and recovery into one experience people could trust.

## A card experience, end to end

Victor led the virtual debit card from architecture through launch. The system paired WebAuthn PRF-derived smart-wallet ownership with KYC, issuance, funding, 3DS authentication, lifecycle controls, provider webhooks, and durable workflows.

The product surface stayed direct while the implementation managed the details users should never have to understand.

## One product across web and native

A shared TypeScript foundation connected Next.js on the web with Expo and React Native. The team moved beyond a PWA, shipped 15 public iOS releases in under eight months, and reached more than 1,000 Android downloads.

Identity experiences supported more than 70,000 passkeys and 20,000 registered Sendtags.

## Reliability as product design

Core payment execution moved to fault-tolerant Temporal workflows coordinating ERC-4337 transactions, confirmations, reconciliation, retries, idempotency, activity updates, and notifications.

That orchestration supported a platform with more than $53 million in onchain transfer volume. It also gave the product a coherent answer when the happy path broke.

## Social money, not a ledger

Group payments, conversational sends, public activity, reactions, rich transaction details, and cross-platform notifications made transactions feel connected to people. Deterministic Storybook scenes let the team reproduce complex states without depending on live backends.
