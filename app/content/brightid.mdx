## From weekend build to public service

The BrightID Discord bot started as a hackathon prototype. Victor took ownership of the path from a useful demonstration to production infrastructure.

## Verification where communities already worked

The bot brought identity verification into Discord, reducing the distance between a community interaction and the BrightID network behind it. In one year, the service verified more than 11,000 people.

## Operating the whole path

Victor migrated deployment from Heroku to Railway and operated a dedicated BrightID node. That meant the product promise depended on infrastructure he could observe, maintain, and improve.

## The durable lesson

Public-good software earns trust through continuity. The most important move was not the prototype itself, but turning it into a service communities could keep using after the event ended.
